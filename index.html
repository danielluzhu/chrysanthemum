Chrysanthemum

Chinese songs, line by line — characters, pinyin, and English together. Put the recording on, follow along, and pick up some Chinese on the way.

Full text is given only for songs in the public domain — classical poems and traditional folk songs. Songs still under copyright are listed with a link and a vocabulary sheet instead of their lyrics; open them on a licensed service to read along.

English translations are original to this project.
