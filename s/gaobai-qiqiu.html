告白氣球

Gàobái Qìqiú

Love Confession

Jay Chou 周杰倫 · contemporary

Vocabulary onlybeginner

Jay Chou's 2016 hit, lyrics by his long-time collaborator Vincent Fang — the same pairing behind [[ju-hua-tai]], working in a completely different register. Where 菊花台 is archaic and mournful, this is light, modern, and set in a Paris of the imagination: a street corner, a café, a balloon.

Under copyright, so no lyrics here. Use the video and read the words on a licensed service — QQ&#38899;&#20048;, Apple Music, Spotify, or YouTube's captions.

For a beginner this is one of the friendliest songs on the list. The vocabulary is everyday, the sentences are short, and Chou articulates far more clearly here than in his faster rap-influenced tracks.

What to listen for

A gentle bossa nova lilt — brushed drums, clean guitar, accordion colouring the verses to sell the Paris setting. The tempo never pushes.

The structure is plain verse–chorus with no bridge to trip you up. Verses sit low and conversational; the chorus lifts and opens. Once you have heard the chorus once you will recognise every later return of it.

Listen for how often the sentences are only four or five syllables. That is unusual in Mandopop and it is what makes the song trackable on a first listen — you get a small complete thought, then a pause, then another.

Grammar patterns

想要 + verb
want to — softer and more wistful than plain 要
我想要去巴黎 I want to go to Paris
让我 + verb
let me — a request or an invitation
让我看看 Let me have a look
一直 + verb
continuously, all along — action sustained over time
我一直在等你 I have been waiting for you all along
A 比 B + adjective
comparison frame: A is more … than B
今天比昨天冷 Today is colder than yesterday
的 + noun
the all-purpose linker: possession and description alike
你的微笑 your smile
