静夜思

Jìng Yè Sī

Quiet Night Thoughts

李白 (Li Bai) · Tang dynasty, c. 8th century

Full textbeginner

Probably the most memorized poem in the Chinese language — most children in China learn it before they can write. Twenty characters, four lines, and almost every one of them is a word a beginner already needs. It has been set to music countless times, so there are many recordings to pick from.

The whole poem turns on a single physical gesture: head up, head down. Moon, then home.

Line by line

床前明月光
Chuáng qián míng yuè guāng
Before my bed, the bright moonlight
疑是地上霜
Yí shì dì shàng shuāng
I took it for frost upon the ground
举头望明月
Jǔ tóu wàng míng yuè
I lift my head and gaze at the bright moon
低头思故乡
Dī tóu sī gùxiāng
I lower my head and think of home
