丑八怪

Chǒubāguài

Ugly

Joker Xue 薛之謙 · contemporary

Vocabulary onlyadvanced

Joker Xue 薛之謙 is one of the most distinctive songwriters in mainland Chinese pop, and this is among his best-known songs. The title is worth understanding before you listen: 丑八怪 is a blunt colloquial insult, roughly "ugly freak" — not a poetic word at all, which is exactly why using it as a song title lands.

Under copyright, so no lyrics here. Use the video and read the words on a licensed service.

The number eight in 丑八怪 is not doing arithmetic. It appears in several Chinese insults as an intensifier, a pattern worth recognising when you meet it elsewhere.

What to listen for

Xue has a nasal, slightly frayed tone that divides listeners. It is deliberate, and it carries the self-deprecation the song is built on.

The arrangement is a straightforward pop-rock ballad, which leaves the vocal exposed. Good news for a learner: there is little production between you and the words.

Listen for the contrast between the low, muttered verses and the chorus opening up. Xue writes this contrast into most of his songs, and once you hear it here you will recognise it in [[shenshi]] and [[tianwai-laiwu]] too.
