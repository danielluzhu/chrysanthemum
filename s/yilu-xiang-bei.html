一路向北

Yīlù Xiàng Běi

All the Way North

Jay Chou 周杰倫 · contemporary

Vocabulary onlyintermediate

The second Jay Chou track here, and a very different one from [[gaobai-qiqiu]]. Where that song is light and playful, this is a slow ballad — and it sits closer in mood to [[ju-hua-tai]], though in plain modern Mandarin rather than Vincent Fang's archaic register.

Under copyright, so no lyrics here. Use the video and read the words on a licensed service.

The title is a compact travel phrase: 一路 ("the whole way") + 向北 ("towards the north"). 向 marks direction and is worth having — 向前 forward, 向上 upward, 向左 to the left.

Plays from YouTube · open there instead

What to listen for

Piano and strings, slow, with Chou singing much more clearly than in his faster material. His diction is often criticised as mumbled; this is a good counter-example.

Compare with [[gaobai-qiqiu]] to hear the range in one artist: bright bossa nova against a dark ballad, and quite different vocal placement in each.

Listen for the guitar line that recurs between sections. It functions as the song's landmark, much as the guzheng figure does in [[ju-hua-tai]].
