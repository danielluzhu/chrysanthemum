水调歌头·明月几时有

Shuǐ Diào Gē Tóu · Míng Yuè Jǐ Shí Yǒu

When Will the Bright Moon Appear?

苏轼 (Su Shi) · Song dynasty, 1076

Full textadvanced

Written at the Mid-Autumn Festival in 1076, while Su Shi was drunk, far from home, and missing his brother. Nine hundred years later it is still what people quote at the Mid-Autumn Festival.

The poem text is ancient and free to reproduce. Note that the famous *sung* versions use modern melodies that are themselves under copyright — the words below are Su Shi's; the tune you'll hear on the recording belongs to its composer.

The last two lines are the ones everyone knows. If you learn nothing else here, learn those.

明月几时有
Míng yuè jǐ shí yǒu
When did the bright moon first appear?
把酒问青天
Bǎ jiǔ wèn qīng tiān
Wine cup in hand, I ask the blue sky
不知天上宫阙
Bù zhī tiān shàng gōng què
I wonder — in those palaces of heaven
今夕是何年
Jīn xī shì hé nián
what year is it tonight?
我欲乘风归去
Wǒ yù chéng fēng guī qù
I long to ride the wind and return there
又恐琼楼玉宇
Yòu kǒng qióng lóu yù yǔ
yet I fear those towers of jade and marble
高处不胜寒
Gāo chù bù shèng hán
are too cold to bear, so high up
起舞弄清影
Qǐ wǔ nòng qīng yǐng
I rise and dance with my own clear shadow
何似在人间
Hé sì zài rén jiān
what place could match this world of ours?
转朱阁
Zhuǎn zhū gé
It rounds the vermilion pavilion
低绮户
Dī qǐ hù
stoops to the carved window
照无眠
Zhào wú mián
and shines on the sleepless
不应有恨
Bù yīng yǒu hèn
It ought to bear us no grudge
何事长向别时圆
Hé shì cháng xiàng bié shí yuán
so why is it always full when we part?
人有悲欢离合
Rén yǒu bēi huān lí hé
People know sorrow and joy, parting and reunion
月有阴晴圆缺
Yuè yǒu yīn qíng yuán quē
the moon knows dark and clear, waxing and waning
此事古难全
Cǐ shì gǔ nán quán
these things have never once been perfect
但愿人长久
Dàn yuàn rén cháng jiǔ
I wish only that we live long
千里共婵娟
Qiān lǐ gòng chán juān
and share this moon a thousand miles apart
