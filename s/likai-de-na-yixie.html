離開的那一些

Líkāi de Nà Yīxiē

Those Who Left

Eric Chou 周興哲 · contemporary

Vocabulary onlyintermediate

The last of the seven Eric Chou tracks on this playlist. By now the register should be familiar — piano ballad, plainly stated feeling, careful diction.

Under copyright, so no lyrics here. Use the video and read the words on a licensed service.

The title is grammatically interesting. 那一些 means "those ones," and putting 離開的 in front of it turns a verb into a description: *those that left*. Chinese does this constantly — verb plus 的 becomes a noun phrase — and it is one of the highest-value patterns a learner can internalise.

What to listen for

Standard ballad architecture, so use this one to test yourself rather than to learn the shape. If you have worked through the other Chou entries here, try following it once with pinyin off.

Chou's habit of holding the final syllable of a phrase is especially clear here. Those held notes are where tones are most audible in sung Mandarin — melody overrides tone almost everywhere else, but a long note lets it through.

Grammar patterns

Verb + 的
turns a verb into a noun phrase: "the ones that —"
离开的人 the people who left
那一些
those ones — plural demonstrative
那一些日子 those days
留下
to stay behind; to leave something behind
他留下一封信 He left behind a letter
Verb + 不了
unable to complete the action
我忘不了你 I can't forget you
慢慢 + verb
gradually, slowly
我会慢慢习惯 I'll get used to it gradually
