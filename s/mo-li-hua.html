茉莉花

Mò Lì Huā

Jasmine Flower

Traditional (Jiangsu folk song) · Qing dynasty, 18th century

Full textbeginner

The Chinese folk song best known outside China. A British envoy's attaché transcribed it in the 1790s and published it in London; Puccini later borrowed the melody for *Turandot*. If you have heard one Chinese folk tune, it is this one.

Simple, repetitive, and slow — the best possible first song for a learner. The grammar is plain and the vocabulary is everyday.

Line by line

好一朵美丽的茉莉花
Hǎo yī duǒ měilì de mòlihuā
What a beautiful jasmine flower
芬芳美丽满枝桠
Fēnfāng měilì mǎn zhīyā
Fragrant and lovely, crowding every branch
又香又白人人夸
Yòu xiāng yòu bái rénrén kuā
Sweet-scented and white, praised by everyone
让我来将你摘下
Ràng wǒ lái jiāng nǐ zhāi xià
Let me pick you from the bough
送给别人家
Sòng gěi biérén jiā
and give you to another
茉莉花呀茉莉花
Mòlihuā ya mòlihuā
Jasmine flower, oh jasmine flower
