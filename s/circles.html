Circles

Circles

高爾宣 OSN · contemporary

Another OSN track built on the same Mandarin-English blend as [[without-you]], though the pacing here is slower and the vocal sits further forward.

Under copyright, so no lyrics here. Use the video and read the words on a licensed service.

The going-in-circles metaphor works identically in Chinese and English, which is worth noting: 兜圈子 means literally to circle around, and figuratively to avoid getting to the point. Not every metaphor survives translation this cleanly, and the ones that do are worth collecting.

What to listen for

Slower than most of his catalogue, with space between phrases. If you have found his other tracks too fast, start here.

Listen for the vowel endings. Mandarin has a set of finals — the -ng endings especially — that English speakers routinely flatten, and OSN's relaxed delivery on this track leaves them clearly audible.

The beat stays constant while the vocal phrasing shifts against it. That tension is a hip-hop convention rather than anything specific to Chinese, but it does make word boundaries harder to place. Read along the first time.
