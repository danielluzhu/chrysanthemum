需要人陪

Xūyào Rén Péi

Need Company

Leehom Wang 王力宏 · contemporary

Vocabulary onlyintermediate

Leehom Wang in a starker mode than [[yiran-ai-ni]]. Where that song is a wedding standard, this one is about the plain fact of needing someone around.

Under copyright, so no lyrics here. Use the video and read the words on a licensed service.

The title is a compact piece of grammar: 需要 ("to need") + 人 ("a person") + 陪 ("to accompany"). Chinese happily strings verbs together like this without the infinitives or prepositions English would demand — "need person accompany". The pattern is called a pivot construction and it is everywhere once you notice it.

What to listen for

Sparser production than most Wang tracks — the arrangement leaves gaps, which suits the subject.

Wang's Mandarin is famously precise. He grew up in the United States and learned much of his Chinese formally, which gives his diction an evenness that native speakers sometimes describe as slightly textbook. For a learner that is an advantage.

Listen for the pivot construction recurring. Once you can hear 需要人陪 as three verbs in a row rather than one lump of sound, a lot of other sentences open up.

Grammar patterns

需要 + person + verb
