送元二使安西

Sòng Yuán Èr Shǐ Ān Xī

Seeing Yuan the Second Off to Anxi

王维 (Wang Wei) · Tang dynasty, c. 8th century

Full textintermediate

Set to music as 阳关三叠 (*Yángguān Sān Dié*, "Three Refrains on Yang Pass"), one of the oldest surviving Chinese art songs, usually performed on guqin. A farewell poem for a friend posted to a garrison at the far western frontier — Yang Pass was the last outpost before the desert.

Four lines, and the whole weight of the poem lands on the last one: past this point there is no one left who knows you.

渭城朝雨浥轻尘
Wèi chéng zhāo yǔ yì qīng chén
Morning rain in Weicheng settles the light dust
客舍青青柳色新
Kè shè qīng qīng liǔ sè xīn
By the inn the willows are green, their color new
劝君更尽一杯酒
Quàn jūn gèng jìn yī bēi jiǔ
Let me urge one more cup of wine on you
西出阳关无故人
Xī chū Yáng guān wú gù rén
West of Yang Pass there will be no old friends
