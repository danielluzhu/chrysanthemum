Without You

Without You

高爾宣 OSN · contemporary

Vocabulary onlyintermediate

高爾宣 OSN is one of the most-represented artists on this playlist, and his work sits in a different place from the ballads around it: Taiwanese R&B and hip-hop, with English hooks over Mandarin verses.

Under copyright, so no lyrics here. Use the video and read the words on a licensed service.

That mixing is not an affectation — code-switching between Mandarin and English is ordinary in urban Taiwanese speech, especially among younger speakers, and pop reflects it. For a learner this is genuinely useful: the English gives you footholds while the Mandarin around it moves fast.

What to listen for

Rapped and sung Mandarin behave differently, and this is a good song to hear that in. In sung lines the melody largely overrides the tones. In rapped sections the tones survive much better, because pitch is not being dictated by a tune. Compare the two within this one track.

The English title and hook recur as anchors. Use them to locate yourself, then work outward into the Mandarin either side.

The production is sparse — mostly beat, bass and voice — so there is little competing with the vocal. That makes it more approachable than the density suggests.
