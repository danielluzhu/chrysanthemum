修煉愛情

Xiūliàn Àiqíng

Practising Love

JJ Lin 林俊傑 · contemporary

Vocabulary onlyadvanced

JJ Lin's 2013 single, and one of the more interesting titles in Mandopop: 修煉 is a word from Daoist and Buddhist practice, meaning to cultivate or refine oneself through disciplined effort over years. Applying it to 愛情 frames love as a practice you get better at rather than something that happens to you.

Under copyright, so no lyrics here. Use the video and read the words on a licensed service.

Marked advanced because Lin sings fast and the phrasing is dense — syllables are packed tightly against the beat, and he slides between them. The individual words are not obscure; keeping up is the difficulty.

What to listen for

A driving mid-tempo arrangement with piano and strings, busier than a standard ballad. There is rarely a gap in the vocal line, which is exactly what makes it hard to follow.

Lin has one of the widest ranges in Mandopop and uses it here. Track the melody climbing across each chorus toward a sustained high note near the end.

Try this one twice: once reading along on a licensed lyrics service, once without. The gap between those two experiences will tell you more about your listening level than any test.
