以後別做朋友

Yǐhòu Bié Zuò Péngyǒu

Let's Not Be Friends Anymore

Eric Chou 周興哲 · contemporary

Vocabulary onlyintermediate

Plays from YouTube · open there instead

The ache of being kept close as a friend by someone you wanted more from, and deciding you would rather have nothing. The title is an instruction, not a lament.

Eric Chou's 2015 breakthrough, and the song that established the lane he has worked in ever since: mid-tempo piano ballads about the ache of the friend zone. He appears on this playlist more than any other artist, and this is the one to start with.

Under copyright, so no lyrics here. Use the video and read the words on a licensed service.

Chou enunciates carefully and sits in a comfortable mid-range, so the words are easier to catch than in most ballads. The sentiment is plainly stated rather than buried in metaphor — good for a learner, since what you decode is what is meant.

What to listen for

Piano-led throughout, strings arriving in the second half. There is nothing rhythmically tricky; the interest is entirely in the vocal line.

The title is a complete sentence — an instruction, in fact — and it sits at the end of the chorus rather than the beginning. That is worth noticing, because it means the chorus resolves onto the title instead of announcing it.

Listen for the register shift in the final chorus, where he moves up and the arrangement thickens behind him. Standard Mandopop ballad architecture, executed cleanly enough to be a good template for hearing it elsewhere.
