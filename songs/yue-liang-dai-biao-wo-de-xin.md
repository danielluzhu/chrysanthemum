## notes

Teresa Teng's 1977 recording is probably the single most recognized Chinese love
song anywhere in the world. Slow, gentle, and sung with unusually clear
articulation — which makes it a favourite of learners.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Listen via the link with the lyrics open on a
licensed service alongside.

Grammatically it is a gift for beginners: short sentences, present tense, heavy
repetition, and the core pattern 代表 ("stands for / represents") repeated throughout.
Learn the words below and you will catch a large share of the song on first listen.

## vocab
