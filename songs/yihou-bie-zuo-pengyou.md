## notes

Eric Chou's 2015 breakthrough, and the song that established the lane he has
worked in ever since: mid-tempo piano ballads about the ache of the friend zone.
He appears on this playlist more than any other artist, and this is the one to
start with.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Use the video and read the words on a
licensed service.

Chou enunciates carefully and sits in a comfortable mid-range, so the words are
easier to catch than in most ballads. The sentiment is plainly stated rather than
buried in metaphor — good for a learner, since what you decode is what is meant.

## listen for

Piano-led throughout, strings arriving in the second half. There is nothing
rhythmically tricky; the interest is entirely in the vocal line.

The title is a complete sentence — an instruction, in fact — and it sits at the
end of the chorus rather than the beginning. That is worth noticing, because it
means the chorus resolves onto the title instead of announcing it.

Listen for the register shift in the final chorus, where he moves up and the
arrangement thickens behind him. Standard Mandopop ballad architecture, executed
cleanly enough to be a good template for hearing it elsewhere.

## patterns

以後 | from now on, afterwards — sets a time frame for what follows | 以后我会小心 | From now on I'll be careful
別 + verb | don't — the negative imperative | 别走 | Don't go
做朋友 | to be friends — 做 for taking on a role or relationship | 我们做朋友吧 | Let's be friends
不能 + verb | cannot, must not — inability or prohibition | 我不能再等了 | I can't wait any longer
把 + object + verb | the 把 construction: do something to a specified object | 把门关上 | Close the door
