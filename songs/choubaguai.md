## notes

Joker Xue 薛之謙 is one of the most distinctive songwriters in mainland Chinese
pop, and this is among his best-known songs. The title is worth understanding
before you listen: 丑八怪 is a blunt colloquial insult, roughly "ugly freak" —
not a poetic word at all, which is exactly why using it as a song title lands.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Use the video and read the words on a
licensed service.

The number eight in 丑八怪 is not doing arithmetic. It appears in several
Chinese insults as an intensifier, a pattern worth recognising when you meet
it elsewhere.

## listen for

Xue has a nasal, slightly frayed tone that divides listeners. It is deliberate,
and it carries the self-deprecation the song is built on.

The arrangement is a straightforward pop-rock ballad, which leaves the vocal
exposed. Good news for a learner: there is little production between you and
the words.

Listen for the contrast between the low, muttered verses and the chorus opening
up. Xue writes this contrast into most of his songs, and once you hear it here
you will recognise it in [[shenshi]] and [[tianwai-laiwu]] too.

## patterns

丑 | ugly | 他觉得自己很丑 | He thinks he's ugly
八 as intensifier | the number 8 turns up inside several set insults | 乱七八糟 | in complete disorder
配不上 | not good enough for, unworthy of | 我配不上你 | I'm not good enough for you
其实 | actually — introduces a correction to what was assumed | 其实我很在乎 | Actually I care a lot
假装 | to pretend | 我假装不在意 | I pretend not to mind
