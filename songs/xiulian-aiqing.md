## notes

JJ Lin's 2013 single, and one of the more interesting titles in Mandopop:
修煉 is a word from Daoist and Buddhist practice, meaning to cultivate or refine
oneself through disciplined effort over years. Applying it to 愛情 frames love as
a practice you get better at rather than something that happens to you.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Use the video and read the words on a
licensed service.

Marked advanced because Lin sings fast and the phrasing is dense — syllables are
packed tightly against the beat, and he slides between them. The individual words
are not obscure; keeping up is the difficulty.

## listen for

A driving mid-tempo arrangement with piano and strings, busier than a standard
ballad. There is rarely a gap in the vocal line, which is exactly what makes it
hard to follow.

Lin has one of the widest ranges in Mandopop and uses it here. Track the melody
climbing across each chorus toward a sustained high note near the end.

Try this one twice: once reading along on a licensed lyrics service, once
without. The gap between those two experiences will tell you more about your
listening level than any test.

## patterns

修煉 | to cultivate, to train through long practice — Daoist/Buddhist in origin | 修炼多年 | to have practised for many years
才 | only then — marks that something took effort or came late | 我现在才明白 | Only now do I understand
過 | verb suffix marking past experience: have ever done | 我去过北京 | I have been to Beijing
越來越 + adjective | more and more — an increasing degree | 天气越来越冷 | The weather is getting colder and colder
把…變成… | turn one thing into another | 把眼泪变成力量 | Turn tears into strength
