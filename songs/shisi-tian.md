## notes

A Chou ballad built around a specific span of time rather than a feeling — the
title is simply a count of days, which is unusual and gives the song a concrete
frame most Mandopop avoids.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Use the video and read the words on a
licensed service.

Useful for a learner precisely because of that number: 十四天 is a clean
demonstration of how Chinese counts days, and the song gives you the phrase
repeatedly in context until it sticks.

## listen for

The now-familiar Chou architecture — sparse piano opening, strings arriving in
the second half, a final chorus that lifts. If you have worked through
[[yihou-bie-zuo-pengyou]] and [[yong-bu-shilian-de-ai]] you will recognise the
shape immediately, which is the point: hearing the same structure across three
songs is what makes the fourth one easy.

Mid-tempo, steady, no rhythmic surprises. Good for a first attempt at following
without reading along.

## patterns

Number + 天 | counting days — 天 needs no measure word, unlike most nouns | 十四天 | fourteen days
过去 | to pass (of time); the past | 三年过去了 | Three years have passed
才 | only then, not until — marks something as later than expected | 我昨天才知道 | I didn't find out until yesterday
还没 + verb | not yet — ongoing incompleteness | 我还没准备好 | I'm not ready yet
从…到… | from … to … — spans of time or space | 从早到晚 | from morning to night
