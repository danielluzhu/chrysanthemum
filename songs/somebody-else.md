## notes

The third of five OSN tracks here, and the one whose subject — someone has moved
on to another person — gives you a specific and very reusable piece of grammar:
别人 and 另一个人.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Use the video and read the words on a
licensed service.

别人 means "other people" in general, while 另一个人 is "another specific
person." English blurs these under "somebody else"; Chinese does not, and
getting the distinction right is one of those small things that makes a sentence
sound native.

## listen for

Mid-tempo R&B with a heavier low end than [[circles]]. The vocal is
double-tracked in the chorus, which thickens it and makes individual words
harder to pick out — expect the verses to be more legible than the hook.

OSN slides between notes a lot. That portamento is characteristic of the genre
but it smears syllable boundaries, so this is not the track to test yourself on
first. Come to it after [[circles]] and [[without-you]].

Listen for where English appears. It tends to land on the emotional peaks rather
than being distributed evenly, which is typical of how code-switching works in
practice — the switch itself carries emphasis.

## patterns

别人 | other people, someone else (general) | 别管别人怎么想 | Don't worry what others think
另一个 | another (specific one) | 他找到了另一个人 | He found someone else
取代 | to replace, to take the place of | 没有人能取代你 | No one can replace you
把…忘了 | to forget something — the 把 construction | 把我忘了吧 | Just forget me
現在 | now, at present | 我现在明白了 | I understand now
