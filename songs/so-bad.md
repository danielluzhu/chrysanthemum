## notes

The fastest of the OSN tracks collected here, and the one to leave until last.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Use the video and read the words on a
licensed service.

Worth using as a benchmark rather than a lesson. If you can follow the verses
here without reading along, your listening has moved well past where the ballads
on this playlist will stretch you.

## listen for

Dense, quick delivery with syllables packed tightly against the beat. Mandarin
is a syllable-timed language — each syllable takes roughly equal time, unlike
English where stressed syllables dominate — and fast rap makes that unusually
audible. Listen for the evenness of the pulse.

Because the pace leaves no room for held notes, the tones flatten considerably.
This is the opposite end of the spectrum from [[yiran-ai-ni]], where sustained
notes let every tone through. Hearing both extremes is more instructive than
either alone.

The English hook is your anchor. Everything else moves fast enough that you will
want the lyrics open on a licensed service the first few times.

## patterns

好 + adjective | so, really — colloquial intensifier | 我好累 | I'm so tired
Verb + 得 + adverb | describes how an action is done | 他跑得很快 | He runs very fast
真的 | really, truly | 我真的不知道 | I really don't know
差点 | almost, nearly (something didn't happen) | 我差点忘了 | I almost forgot
到底 | after all, on earth — adds urgency to a question | 你到底想要什么？ | What on earth do you want?
