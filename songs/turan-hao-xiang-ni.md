## notes

Mayday are the biggest rock band in the Mandarin-speaking world, and this 2008
song is among their most-sung. Written for the film 《海角七號》 era of Taiwanese
pop, it has outlasted its moment entirely.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Use the video and read the words on a
licensed service.

Ashin's diction is clear and the tempo is moderate, which makes this more
approachable than the band's faster material. The vocabulary is plain modern
Mandarin — the challenge is that phrases run long, so you need to hold a clause
in your head while the next arrives.

## listen for

A slow build across four and a half minutes, from near-silence to a full-band
final chorus with the crowd audibly expected to join in. Live recordings make
that obvious; the studio version only implies it.

The title phrase is the hook and lands at the top of the chorus. Four syllables,
easy to catch — use it to orient yourself.

Note the gap between how gentle the verses are and how much the chorus opens.
Mayday build almost everything this way, and once you hear the pattern you can
predict the shape of most of their catalogue.

## patterns

突然 | suddenly — abrupt change, placed before the verb | 我突然想起来了 | I suddenly remembered
好 + verb | intensifier: really, so — colloquial, very common in songs | 我好想你 | I miss you so much
想 + person | to miss someone (distinct from 想 + verb, "want to") | 我想我妈妈 | I miss my mother
还是 | still, nevertheless — persistence despite everything | 我还是喜欢你 | I still like you
如果…就… | if … then … — the standard conditional frame | 如果下雨，我就不去 | If it rains, I won't go
