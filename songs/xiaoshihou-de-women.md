## notes

A nostalgia song — Chou looking back at childhood rather than at a relationship,
which makes it a change of subject from most of his catalogue on this playlist.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Use the video and read the words on a
licensed service.

The grammar is a good showcase for how Chinese handles the past. There is no
tense conjugation, so pastness is carried by time words like 小時候 ("when
[we] were small") and aspect markers like 了 and 過. This song leans on the
first method, which is the easiest to hear.

## listen for

Gentler and more mid-tempo than his big ballads, with a lighter arrangement —
acoustic guitar carries more of it than piano does.

The title phrase 小時候的我們 is a complete noun phrase meaning "the us of
childhood," and hearing how 的 links a time expression to a pronoun is the
single most transferable thing in the song. Once that clicks, constructions like
去年的我 ("the me of last year") come free.

Listen for the shift when the song moves from the past to the present. The
arrangement changes underneath it.

## patterns

小時候 | when (someone) was young — a time word, no tense needed | 我小时候住在北京 | I lived in Beijing as a child
Time + 的 + pronoun | "the [us] of [then]" — links a period to a person | 去年的我 | the me of last year
以前 | before, formerly | 我以前不喜欢咖啡 | I didn't like coffee before
Verb + 過 | have ever done — experiential aspect | 我去过那个学校 | I've been to that school
再也不 + verb | never again | 我再也不会忘记 | I'll never forget again
