## notes

Joker Xue again, and a title that carries more weight in Chinese than the
English "gentleman" suggests. 紳士 is a borrowed concept — the word entered
Chinese via Japanese in the modern era to describe a Western-style gentleman —
so it arrives already carrying an air of performance and restraint.

**Under copyright, so no lyrics here.** Use the video and read the words on a
licensed service.

That performed restraint is the subject: behaving well while feeling anything
but. If you have listened to [[choubaguai]] you will recognise the same
self-aware register.

## listen for

Piano-driven and more polished than [[choubaguai]], with strings arriving in the
back half. Xue's nasal edge is still there but reined in.

Watch the dynamics rather than the words on a first pass. The song is built as a
long controlled build with one release near the end, which mirrors what it is
describing.

The phrasing is unhurried and the diction clear, making this the most
approachable of the three Xue tracks on this playlist. Start here if you are
new to him.
